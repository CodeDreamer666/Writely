# Writing workspace
Mode: Operate. Visual authority: the exact nine-page PDF brief.
## Direction contract
THESIS: The prose owns the screen; revision is an intention, not a separate editor. No dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Exact warm paper and dark tokens, teal actions, Source Serif 4 prose and Inter controls. Thought-card colours extracted directly from PDF vector fills.
STORY: Open an unfinished essay, capture a note, choose one check, edit, preserve a named draft, inspect differences, explicitly finish.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim left document rail, quiet top navigation, centred 680px writing column, restrained document title and prose; two small notes in the surrounding whitespace. A bottom writing-status line and discreet document tools.
FORM: Brief-pinned centred writing surface; no randomized visual replacement applies to the prescribed system.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
